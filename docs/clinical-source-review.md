# Clinical source audit

Reviewed September 21, 2026. Scope: both procedure modules (Overview, Anatomy, Steps, Complications, Pimp and Quiz), including answer explanations, pearls and definition tooltips. This is an educational source audit, not independent clinical faculty approval.

## Corrections made separately from citation UI

- **Retrocecal frequency:** removed the conflicting 30% and 65% claims; the quiz now asks about location. A cadaveric study illustrates population-specific estimates, not a universal replacement percentage: [Patra et al., 2024; online 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37691509/).
- **CODA:** corrected the endpoint to 30-day health status and subsequent appendectomy to 29% by 90 days (41% with an appendicolith, 25% without). Removed the unsupported one-year success claim and ambiguous quiz distractor. [CODA, NEJM 2020](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2014320).
- **Appendicitis treatment:** removed universal mandates for irrigation, drainage, and omission of interval surgery. SAGES conditionally supports either suction alone or lavage and favors interval appendectomy in adults after complicated appendicitis. Patient selection and local expertise matter. [SAGES guideline](https://www.sages.org/publications/guidelines/guideline-for-the-diagnosis-and-treatment-of-appendicitis/). The [2025 WSES edition, published 2026](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamasurg.2025.6218) supports selected antibiotic treatment and follow-up for neoplasm after abscess management; only its accessible abstract was used, not inaccessible recommendation details.
- **Normal appendix and Alvarado:** removed unconditional removal and stand-alone diagnostic scoring. The normal-appendix teaching point explicitly identifies the weak, conditional [WSES 2020 recommendation 5.3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7386163/).
- **Appendiceal NET / mucinous neoplasm:** removed the blanket claim that every tumor at most 2 cm is cured and that any base involvement mandates hemicolectomy. Management requires staging, pathology and specialist assessment. Removed uncited incidence/metastasis percentages. [ASCRS 2025, recommendations 7 and 14-15](https://www.unboundmedicine.com/ascrs/view/ASCRS-Evidence-Based-Guidelines-and-Expert-Consensus/3982016/all/Management_of_Appendiceal_Neoplasms__2025_).
- **Trocar closure:** changed “required” to “suggested,” matching the weak recommendation and limited evidence in [EHS/AHS 2022, KQ2](https://doi.org/10.1093/bjs/znac302).
- **Friable base:** replaced a categorical device prohibition with tissue-viability assessment. [Partial cecal resection study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8886479/) is observational evidence, not a universal technique mandate.
- **Cholecystectomy:** aligned the Overview with same-admission treatment after mild biliary pancreatitis improves, clarified hepatocystic triangle terminology in questions, and expanded the Anatomy CVS reminder to all three requirements. Diagram selection keys remain intact. [AAST guideline summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/6461136/), [SAGES multi-society guideline](https://www.sages.org/publications/guidelines/safe-cholecystectomy-multi-society-practice-guideline/).
- **SSI:** distinguish drainage from selective adjunctive antibiotics. [IDSA, surgical site infection section](https://www.idsociety.org/practice-guideline/skin-and-soft-tissue-infections/).

## Remaining medical review considerations

- Interval appendectomy and management of a normal-looking appendix vary with age, clinical scenario, guideline and evidence certainty. The site states those limits instead of presenting one universal policy.
- Rare ureter injury and stump-leak sources include case reports; those references demonstrate a reported complication, not its frequency or a universal treatment algorithm.
- Complication timeframes are broad teaching descriptions, not diagnostic cutoffs. Management entries are summaries; patient-specific treatment is outside this site's scope.
- Technique sources describe accepted approaches, not an exclusive sequence or a fixed number of clips/loops.

## Citation maintenance

Sources must sit beside the corresponding overview section, step, complication row, selected anatomy note, or revealed answer. Quiz citations appear after submission to avoid revealing answers. Every question has a required typed source list. Keep original illustration and photo credits distinct from clinical references. Check the cited section before editing a medical claim; do not attach a broad reference to unsupported details.

### Clinical decisions and verification

- Retained appendiceal base, McBurney point, positions, blood supply, obstruction pathway, pain migration, and examination maneuvers. Checked appendix anatomy and appendicitis chapters in StatPearls and Deshmukh et al. (2014). Do not present obstruction as an inevitable pathway to perforation. Omitted the document's McBurney sensitivity/specificity ranges because the exact estimates could not be verified in the accessible Moris review. Omitted a universal ranking or percentage for tip positions, consistent with the earlier variation audit.
- Examination signs are supportive rather than diagnostic; Yale et al. (2022), especially its obturator description, supports technique and limitations. Its pregnancy-focused review is not used as a population-wide accuracy estimate.
- Laboratory testing is framed as assessment, not as proof of appendicitis or automatic exclusion of ectopic pregnancy/UTI. NIDDK diagnosis page and WSES 2020 diagnostic sections support these points.
- Imaging is conditional on patient group and clinical risk. ACR Right Lower Quadrant Pain (2022), variants 2 and 3, supports IV-contrast CT for nonpregnant adults and US or noncontrast MRI in pregnancy. WSES 2020 recommendations 1.13.2 and 1.14.1 support MRI after inconclusive US in pregnancy and US-first pediatric assessment. SAGES 2024 KQ1 recognizes ultrasound-first pathways and local-resource considerations. Avoid calling CT the universal first test in every adult.
- Alvarado weights checked directly against MDCalc; WSES 2020 recommendations 1.3 to 1.5 support risk stratification and limitations. The calculator link is an external resource, not a stand-alone diagnostic recommendation.
- Abscess care is not universally drainage instead of surgery. WSES 2020 recommendations 6.1 and 6.2 and SAGES 2024 KQ3 allow case selection and laparoscopic expertise to affect management. WSES 2025 edition (2026) abstract supports follow-up after nonoperative abscess care. The full newer recommendation text was not available; no specific newer age cutoff or interval-surgery mandate was inferred.
- CODA is cited to the primary 2020 trial, verified through PubMed PMID 33017106, rather than the document's 2021 review. Preserved 30-day EQ-5D noninferiority and 90-day appendectomy outcomes (29%, 41% with appendicolith, 25% without). Did not generalize these as long-term cure rates.
